Derived from the 1k JPEG release (Diffuse and Rough), downsampled to
512x512. The diffuse also has a per-channel gamma lift applied
(r 0.613, g 0.538, b 0.504): the scan is a dark stained walnut and the
desk wants a warm *medium* tone, which puts the channel means on
roughly #6b4a33. The grain runs up the image; the material rotates the
UVs a quarter turn so it runs along the desk's length instead.

Both are only ever seen at a few hundred pixels across, so 512 costs
nothing visually and ~390 kB of page weight against the 1k originals.
